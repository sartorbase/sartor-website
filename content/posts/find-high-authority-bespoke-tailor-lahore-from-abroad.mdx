## 2. The 2.5-Inch Internal Seam Allowance: Your Overseas Fit Insurance

When ordering clothes from abroad, the single greatest technical safeguard is the **internal seam margin**.

```
            SARTOR 2.5-INCH FINISHED SEAM ANATOMY
 
        [ Outer Fashion Silk Fabric ]
                     |
        [ Primary Master Stitch Line ]  <-- Laser-Aligned Fitting
                     |
        [ 2.5" Overlocked Silk Seam Margin ]  <-- Alteration Buffer
                     |
        [ Hand-Finished Blind Slip-Stitch ]
```

### Why Commercial Tailors Cut Seams Narrow
In commercial retail bazaars, tailors cut fabric aggressively to maximize leftover yardage, which they resell as scrap patches. They leave barely 6 millimeters (0.25 inches) of raw, frayed fabric beyond the stitch line. 

When your suit arrives in London or Toronto:
* A local alteration tailor cannot let the garment out even by half a centimeter.
* The raw edges fray and pull apart under tension when seated.
* Dry cleaners’ steam presses cause the narrow seam ridges to show through delicate silks.

### The SARTOR 2.5-Inch Protocol
Every kurti, blouse, and trouser crafted at SARTOR features:
1. **Full 2.5-Inch Margins:** Generous fabric allowance preserved inside both side seams, sleeve insets, and trouser outseams.
2. **Double-Overlocked Silk Edges:** All raw edges are machine-overlocked with color-matched silk thread, preventing fraying during dry cleaning.
3. **Multi-Stage Stitch Guide:** An interior basting guide that allows any local tailor abroad to release stitches cleanly without leaving needle puncture holes in raw silk, velvet, or organza.

> Artisan Note:
> "We cut garments with the understanding that a client in Houston or London cannot pop into our atelier for a minor tweak," explains Master Tailor Abdul Ghaffar. "A 2.5-inch seam allowance guarantees that your garment adapts to your body across years, life stages, and seasonal weight fluctuations without compromising structural integrity."
