# Heritage Craftsmanship Meets Modern Couture

For discerning Pakistani women living in the **United Kingdom, United States, Canada, the UAE, and Australia**, procuring master-crafted traditional attire has historically been filled with uncertainty:

* Sizing inaccuracies resulting in unwearable garments upon international arrival.
* Machine-embossed polyester traded off as authentic zardozi or dabka handwork.
* Endless chasing of local bazaars through relatives with zero visual accountability.

**Sartor was founded to transform this experience entirely.**

---

## 1. Direct Access to Lahore’s Master Karigars

Every garment commissioned through Sartor is drafted and stitched in our private Lahore studio. We do not outsource to unvetted wholesale factories. 

Our team comprises generational master ustads specialized in:

1. **Zardozi & Kora Dabka**: Heavy metallic wire needlework applied on weighted pure silks, tissue, and organza.
2. **Marori & Resham Threadwork**: Fluid floral creepers and jaal patterns executed with pure silk floss.
3. **Mukesh & Kaamdani Pin-Work**: Authentic flattened metallic wire hammered into chiffons.
4. **Structured Kalidar & Lehenga Architecture**: Multi-panel flared cuts with horsehair canvas hems, can-can underpinnings, and micro-piping.

```markdown
"Bespoke tailoring is not merely sewing pieces of cloth together; it is an architectural study of silhouette, drape, and heirloom longevity."
```

## 3. How We Guarantee a Flawless Overseas Fit

We have refined an international measurement protocol that eliminates the need for in-person fittings:

* **1-on-1 Guided Video Consultation**: Our senior master tailor walks you through 28 distinct anatomical measurements over WhatsApp Video.
* **Internal 2.5-Inch Margin Allowance**: Every couture bodice and lehenga waistband includes built-in finished seam allowances, allowing local expansion or tapering without unpicking embroidery.
* **Double-Layered Interfacing**: Keeps bridal bodices structured without digging into the shoulders or ribcage during long wedding festivities.
