## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is SARTOR Atelier located, and do you offer Lahore pickup?
SARTOR Atelier is located at Shop F-9, Moon Tower, Block 30-M, International Market, Model Town, Lahore, Pakistan. We provide complimentary doorstep fabric pickup and finished garment delivery across Model Town, DHA, Gulberg, Cantt, Johar Town, and Bahria Town.

### How does SARTOR fit overseas clients in the UK, USA, Canada, and UAE without in-person visits?
Clients log baseline figures in our pre-beta digital measurement portal at [app.sartor.pk](https://app.sartor.pk) and complete a 20-minute guided WhatsApp video fitting with Master Tailor Abdul Ghaffar, backed by 1.5–2.5 inch finished internal alteration margins.

### Can I send unstitched designer suits from brands like Maria.B or Elan straight to SARTOR?
Yes. Overseas buyers ship online parcels directly from Pakistani designer checkouts to our Model Town, Lahore atelier for quality inspection, bespoke cutting, and consolidated DHL Express international delivery.

### What is SARTOR's turnaround time and payment structure?
Precision stitching requires 7–10+ business days depending on seasonal queue and garment complexity, while full *adda* hand-embroidered bridals span 8–14 weeks. Payments follow a secure 4-step milestone schedule: 10% booking, 30% swatch approval, 30% mid-production, and 30% final dispatch.
