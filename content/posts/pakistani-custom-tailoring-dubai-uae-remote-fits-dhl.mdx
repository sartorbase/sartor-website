## 2. Engineering Garments for the Gulf Climate: Fabrics & Linings

Tailoring for Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or Doha requires specialized structural knowledge that Western dressmakers and commercial high-street tailors overlook. 

The UAE climate oscillates between 42°C outdoor humidity and chilled 19°C air-conditioned offices and ballrooms. If an atelier lines a silk chiffon or organza suit with cheap polyester crepe, perspiration cannot evaporate. The garment becomes an uncomfortable heat trap and develops unsightly underarm moisture rings.

### The SARTOR Breathability Protocol
* **Zero Synthetic Linings:** We pair pure silks, organzas, and chiffons exclusively with ultra-fine cotton lawn (*malmal*) or lightweight 100% Habotai silk.
* **Pre-Shrinking & Grain Balancing (*Charak*):** All base fabrics and lining materials undergo warm water immersion and grain-squaring before shears touch cloth. Your trousers and kurtis will never shrink or twist after their first dry clean in Dubai.
* **Micro-Woven Neckline Interfacings:** We utilize soft, featherlight German woven interfacings for collars and neckbands. They flex naturally against the collarbone without blistering, curling, or turning into stiff cardboard under high-humidity steam irons.

> **Artisan Note:** When stitching pure raw silk or katan kalidars for indoor gala events at the Burj Al Arab or Emirates Palace, we integrate hidden sweat-shields crafted from absorbent pure cotton beneath the armholes. This protects delicate metallic zardozi threads from perspiration salts, preventing the gold wire from tarnishing over time.
