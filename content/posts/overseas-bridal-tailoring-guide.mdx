# Crafting Your Dream Bridal Outfit From Overseas

Commissioning your Baraat, Walima, or Mehndi ensemble from London, Toronto, Houston, or Sydney no longer requires frantic transatlantic phone calls or guessing your bust measurement on a chart.

Here is Sartor's comprehensive blueprint for a stress-free custom bridal commissioning process.

---

## 1. When Should You Start? (The Ideal Timeline)

Hand-embroidery on high-density bridal lehengas takes hundreds of karigar hours:

* **Bridal Baraat Lehenga / Gown (Heavy Handwork)**: 8 to 14 weeks.
* **Walima Farshi Gharara or Tissue Peshwas**: 6 to 10 weeks.
* **Mehndi / Sangeet Flared Kalidar**: 4 to 6 weeks.
* **Formal Festive / Trousseau Suits**: 2 to 4 weeks.

We always recommend factoring in **5 to 7 business days** for DHL or FedEx International Priority shipping with tracking and temperature-controlled handling.

---

## 2. Deciding Between Fabric Textures

Fabric selection defines how your bridal silhouette hangs under evening banquet lighting:

1. **Pure Katan Silk & Rawa Silk**: Stiff, regal, and ideal for heavy panels bearing 8-10 kg of stone and metallic embroidery.
2. **Organza & Tissue**: Ethereal, translucent sheen that catches chandelier sparkle without adding unneeded bulk.
3. **French Net & Chiffon**: Perfect for fluid dupattas that drape effortlessly over the shoulder and head without slipping.

---

## 3. The Adda Swatch Guarantee

Before a single inch of your final bridal fabric is cut, our artisans prepare an **exact 8" x 8" sample swatch** directly on our wooden embroidery frame (adda). 

You receive a 4K macro video showcasing:
* Exact thread shade matching against natural daylight.
* Height of the raised zardozi padding.
* Stone cut and light refraction.

Once you approve the swatch via WhatsApp, production begins with 100% confidence.
