## 2. Placket Engineering & Zero-Gap Waistcoat Collar Alignment

Even the finest pure silk Boski looks untidy if the front button placket (*gala patti*) twists diagonally or if your **Kadhwa Banarsi Jamawar waistcoat** separates awkwardly from the kurta neckband.

Commercial workshops use brittle, glue-heavy cardboard *bakram* inside kurtas and stamp waistcoats from generic flat vest patterns. The result is an awkward hollow triangle visible behind the neck in side-profile wedding photos.

### The Three-Point SARTOR Menswear Standard

1. **Floating Woven Placket Interlining:** We reject stiff fusible plastic *bakram*. SARTOR laminates lightweight German woven hair-canvas interlining along the 1.25-inch front placket and 1.5-inch *sherwani* band collar, keeping the neckline crisp yet supple without chafing the Adam’s apple.
2. **Matched Anatomical Neck Pitch:** Both your Boski kurta band collar and your Jamawar waistcoat lapel gorge are drafted from the exact same forward cervical slope (typically 10°–14° forward pitch), locking the waistcoat snugly around your collar nape during movement.
3. **High Scye-Depth Kurta Armholes:** Rather than cutting cavernous low sleeves that bunch inside a waistcoat, SARTOR raises the armhole *scye* depth to sit 0.75 inches below the natural armpit—giving you effortless freedom to raise your arms without lifting the waistcoat hem.

To see how scye depth prevents garment shifting across tailored silhouettes, consult our technical breakdown of [The Scye Depth Equation](/blog/the-scye-depth-equation-why-saree-blouses-gape-at-the-back).

> **Key Takeaway:** Always commission your Boski kurta and Jamawar waistcoat from the same master pattern cutter rather than buying them separately. Matching shoulder slope and cervical neck pitch eliminates collar gapping completely.

## 3. Shalwar Drape Physics, Pocket Reinforcement & Jamawar Interior Craft

A poorly proportioned shalwar destroys the dignity of a luxury Boski kurta. Off-the-rack menswear sets ship with skimpy, straight-legged pajama cuts that bind across the calves when sitting cross-legged on Nikkah stages or mosque carpets.

Conversely, overly wide ungraded shalwars drag clumsily under leather Peshawari chappals or suede loafers.

### How SARTOR Balances Lower Drape & Waistcoat Structure

* **Ergonomic Shalwar Fork & Weighted *Pauncha*:** We draft a full-ease contoured shalwar fork with 16–18 inches of thigh rotation and stabilize the 1.5-inch ankle cuff (*pauncha*) with pre-shrunk woven tape—creating clean vertical folds above your footwear.
* **Pure Habotai Silk Waistcoat Lining:** Handloom Banarsi Jamawar is backed on the inside with raw metallic *zari* threads that catch onto delicate Boski silk. SARTOR lines every Jamawar waistcoat in smooth 60 GSM pure Habotai mulberry silk with an internal breast welt pocket for your phone or vows card.
* **1.5–2.0-Inch Finished Seam Allowances:** Both the Boski side seams and the Jamawar waistcoat center-back and side-body seams preserve 1.5 to 2.0 inches of finished alteration margin so future weight shifts never retire the ensemble.
* **Hand-Covered Silk Shank Buttons:** Instead of sharp plastic buttons that saw through silk buttonholes, we wrap solid brass cores in matching Jamawar or ivory Katan silk thread (*kapray kay button*).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do commercial Boski kurtas pucker and ripple down the front button placket?
Authentic 8-pound pure mulberry Boski silk lacks horizontal stretch and carries a slippery spun-silk surface. When commercial tailors fuse rigid low-grade polyester buckram onto an unwashed kurta placket using industrial steam presses, the silk warps and the buckram shrinks unevenly after one dry-clean. SARTOR pre-steams raw Boski twice and laminates German floating woven horsehair canvas along the placket so the button line remains crisp and ripple-free for decades.

### What is the difference between genuine 8-pound pure Boski and synthetic China or Poly-Boski?
Authentic 8-pound pure Boski is woven from natural spun mulberry silk (approx. 140–155 GSM), offering cool thermoregulation in humid venues, a matte pearl-like ivory luster, and zero static cling. Commercial Poly-Boski blends polyester microfibers that shine harshly under camera flash, trap perspiration within 30 minutes, and pill along the inner thighs and cuffs.

### How do you prevent a Jamawar waistcoat from gapping away from the Boski band collar?
Waistcoat necklines gap when tailors cut flat shoulder slopes without matching the groom's forward neck pitch and collar height. At SARTOR Model Town Lahore, Master Tailor Abdul Ghaffar grades both the Boski band collar and the waistcoat lapel gorge to the same anatomical neck curvature, anchoring the waistcoat shoulders cleanly over high scye-depth kurta armholes.

### How does SARTOR keep pure ivory Boski Shalwar Kameez from looking sheer or clinging in daylight?
Lower-weight 5-pound or 6-pound Boski turns translucent under outdoor Nikkah sunlight. SARTOR sources verified heavyweight 8-pound pure spun Boski and pairs the shalwar with an ultra-lightweight breathable voile modesty brief lining plus a 1.5-inch pleated ankle cuff (*pauncha*) stabilized with woven cotton-silk tape so the drape falls cleanly without static leg cling.
