## 1. Long Shirt-Lehenga vs. Short *Choli* vs. Open *Peshwas*: Structural Comparison

Choosing between silhouettes comes down to stage seating posture, climate warmth, and how your proportions balance visual jewelry vs. embroidery weight:

| Silhouette Metric | Traditional Short *Choli* Lehenga | Sculpted Long Shirt-Lehenga (*Kameez Lehenga*) | Open-Front Coat / Jacket Lehenga |

> **Artisan Note:** A classic mistake on heavy **100 GSM Katan silk** shirt-lehengas is pinning the *lehenga* waistband loosely around the soft stomach while putting stiff *can-can* mesh right up to the hipbones. When you layer a fitted embroidered *kameez* over *can-can* puffs, the shirt pushes outward like a bell! We always start subtle skirt *can-can* stiffening **13 to 15 inches below the waist** so the upper shirt falls clean, straight, and razor-slender over your hips before the grand flare blooms below.
